# Translations taken from GOV.UK

Where the Government Digital Service has published its own wording for a piece of interface
text, the Welsh and Spanish files here (`strings-cy.xml`, `strings-es.xml`) use it unchanged.
Each file marks which strings those are: its first group.

Source: [govuk_publishing_components](https://github.com/alphagov/govuk_publishing_components),
`config/locales/cy.yml` and `config/locales/es.yml`, at commit `7f411f2` (6 November 2026).

The strings taken are the skip link, "Contents", "Menu", "Search", "Support links",
"Published", "Last updated", "Published by", "Pagination", the Open Government Licence line,
"© Crown copyright", and the accordion's show and hide text. The accordion's two
"this section" labels are put together as GOV.UK does, from its "Show" or "Hide" and its
"this section". Links inside GOV.UK's licence and copyright lines are left out; the plugin
writes its own.

Everything else in those files is this project's translation, apart from the DITA-OT labels in
`strings-es.xml`, which use DITA-OT's Spanish where it has one.
